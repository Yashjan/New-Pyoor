# Horizon

The gold thread: a single 1px `accent-line` rule, the horizon of Sambhar Lake's salt pans, and Pyoor's signature device.

Use `<hr class="py-horizon">` under the hero, at section breaks and as the active-navigation underline. Keep one on screen at a time, full-bleed or container-wide. It may draw in from left to right once (`duration-slow`, `ease-settle`); under reduced motion it simply appears. Never doubled, never a gradient, never decorated.
