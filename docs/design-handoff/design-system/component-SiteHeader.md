# SiteHeader

Three-part header: navigation on the left, the logo centred, search and bag on the right.

On ivory, the logo sits directly on the header. On navy, it sits on the **ivory crest**: a `crest` tab with `radius-crest` bottom corners, like the crest on the jar label, because the green logo must always sit on ivory. Desktop height 84px with a 44px logo; on phones 64px with a 34px logo, with navigation moving into a full-screen menu. The bag is an outline pill showing the item count, "Bag (n)". The header becomes sticky after the hero and gains a `hairline` bottom border.
