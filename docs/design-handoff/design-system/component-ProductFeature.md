# ProductFeature

The product as an object: a large jar render on a `surface-alt` panel, with the Hindi name, Cormorant name, one line of taste and use, facts, price and "Add to bag" beside it.

Pyoor shows products one at a time, alternating the image left and right down the page, instead of a grid of cards. The consumer provides: the jar image (a transparent render from the Pyoor jar models), Hindi and English names, one taste-and-use sentence, a facts line (ingredients · size), and a price or `null`. With no price, replace the primary button with a disabled "Price coming soon" button. Text stays factual: no claims, badges or "best value" tags. On phones the image stacks above the text, and the name drops to display-s (32px).
